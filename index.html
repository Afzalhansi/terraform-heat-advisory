UAE Midday Outdoor Work Ban Advisory

Under UAE Labor Law, outdoor activities under direct sunlight are strictly prohibited between 12:30 PM and 3:00 PM during summer months (June 15 – September 15). Employers must provide shaded rest areas and cold electrolyte drinks.

Emergency Heat Illness Symptom Matrix

Quickly identify and respond to Heat Exhaustion vs. Life-Threatening Heat Stroke.

Dubai Health Authority (DHA) Guidelines

Heat Exhaustion

Urgent Action Required
  • Heavy sweating & cool, pale, clammy skin
  • Fast, weak pulse & muscle cramps
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, or fainting
  • Nausea, vomiting, or persistent headaches
Immediate Action:

Move person to shaded/AC room, sip cool water/electrolytes, unbutton tight clothing, apply cold compresses.

Heat Stroke (CRITICAL)

Call 998 Immediately
  • High body temperature (above 39.5°C)
  • Hot, red, dry, or damp skin (lack of sweating)
  • Rapid, strong pulse & severe confusion/slurred speech
  • Loss of consciousness, seizures, or coma
Emergency Protocol:

Call **998 Ambulance** instantly. Do NOT give fluids if unconscious. Cool the body using ice packs on neck, armpits, and groin.
