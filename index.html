UAE Heat Safety Advisory

Extreme heat conditions are active across the UAE. Follow these safety guidelines:

Midday work ban: Outdoor labor is prohibited 12:30 PM – 3:00 PM.
Hydration: Drink water regularly, even if not thirsty.
Shade: Avoid direct sun exposure during peak hours.
Watch for symptoms: Dizziness, nausea, or confusion may indicate heat exhaustion — stop activity immediately.
